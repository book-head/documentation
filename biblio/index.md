# Selling on Biblio

Bookhead can list your inventory on Biblio, the marketplace for used, rare, and
out-of-print books, making your copies discoverable to collectors and keeping
them in sync as they sell.

This integration is set up with help from our team. To get started, email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net) and we'll help you connect
your Biblio account.
