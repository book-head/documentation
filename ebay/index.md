# Selling on eBay

Bookhead can list your inventory on eBay, creating marketplace listings from the
books in your catalog and keeping quantities in sync as copies sell.

This integration is set up with help from our team. To get started, email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net) and we'll walk you through
connecting your eBay seller account.
