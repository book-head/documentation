# Selling on Alibris

Bookhead can list your inventory on Alibris, the marketplace for used, rare, and
out-of-print books, making your copies available to its buyers and keeping them
in sync as they sell.

This integration is set up with help from our team. To get started, email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net) and we'll help you connect
your Alibris account.
