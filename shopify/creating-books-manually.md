# Creating book products manually

When you only need to add a book or two, you can create products by hand. You
provide a few basics and Bookhead does the rest — looking up the cover and
bibliographic data and writing a finished product to your Shopify store.

## When to use this

Manual creation is best for small, one-off additions:

- A single new arrival that wasn't part of a distributor shipment
- A special order for a customer
- A book you're adding outside your normal receiving flow

For adding many books at once — like a shipment from a distributor — use
[Importing a distributor file](./importing-distributor-file.md) instead. It's far
faster for bulk additions.

## What you provide

Bookhead can build a complete product from just three pieces of information:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ISBN** | Yes | ISBN-10 or ISBN-13. This is how Bookhead looks up the cover and bibliographic data. |
| **Quantity** | Yes | How many copies you have. |
| **Price** | Yes | Your selling price. |

## Steps

1. In Bookhead, choose to create a new book.
2. Enter the **ISBN**, **quantity**, and **price**.
3. Save. Bookhead looks up the **cover image** and **bibliographic data** for that
   ISBN and assembles the product.
4. Bookhead checks your Shopify catalog and then **creates the product** on your
   Shopify store (or updates the matching product if you already carry that
   title, so you don't get a duplicate).

## After creating

Open your Shopify admin to confirm the new product — cover, title, author,
description, price, and quantity. If a brand-new or obscure ISBN came back with
limited data, the product is still created; you can edit it to fill in anything
missing.
