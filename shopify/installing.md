# Installing the app

Bookhead installs onto your Shopify store like any other app from the Shopify App
Store. The whole process takes a few minutes.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- A Shopify store you own or have admin access to
- The ability to install apps on that store

## Install from the Shopify App Store

1. Open the **Bookhead** listing on the Shopify App Store.
2. Click **Add app** (or **Install**).
3. Shopify shows you the permissions Bookhead needs to create and update
   products on your store. Review them and click **Install app** to approve.
4. You're returned to Bookhead to finish connecting your account.

## What happens on first connect

When the app is installed, Bookhead links to your store and does an initial read
of your existing Shopify catalog. This first read is how Bookhead learns what
books are already on your store so it can keep them in sync rather than creating
duplicates. Depending on the size of your catalog, this can take a little while.

Installing the app does **not** publish anything new on its own. Products are
created and updated only after you bring inventory in from a source — see
[Choosing your inventory source](./inventory-source.md).

## After installing

Once the app is connected, set up where your books come from:

- [Choosing your inventory source](./inventory-source.md)
- [Importing a distributor file](./importing-distributor-file.md)

If anything looks off during installation, email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net).
