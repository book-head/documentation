# Selling on channels

Bookhead lists your bookstore's inventory on a range of online sales channels and
keeps each one in sync with your inventory source. Whenever inventory changes at
your store, Bookhead updates your listings on the channels you've connected.

Pick the channel you want to sell on for its setup steps and details.

## Supported channels

- **[Shopify](/shopify)** — full product sync, including collections
- **[Squarespace](/squarespace)** — full product sync with inventory updates
- **[eBay](/ebay)** — marketplace listings for used and rare books
- **[Biblio](/biblio)** — marketplace for used, rare, and out-of-print books
- **[Alibris](/alibris)** — marketplace for used, rare, and out-of-print books

Don't see the channel you want to sell on? Email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net) — we're always adding new
integrations.
