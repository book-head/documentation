# Special orders

Your shelf is finite. Your catalog isn't. When a customer wants a book you don't
have on hand, your staff says the magic words: *we can get that for you.*
Special orders bring that to your website — so you never lose a sale just because
a title wasn't in stock.

## How it works

Alongside the books on your shelves, your store can offer millions of
special-order titles from **Ingram's catalog** — covers, descriptions, and all —
and fulfill them straight from your Ingram warehouse.

1. **Search any title.** If it's not on your shelf, you can still offer it from
   Ingram's catalog.
2. **List it with complete data.** Special-order titles carry Ingram's rich
   metadata — descriptions, subjects, and cover images — so the listing looks as
   good as your in-stock books.
3. **Fulfill from Ingram.** When a customer orders a special-order title, you
   fulfill it from your Ingram warehouse.

## Ingram data

Special orders draw on **Ingram data** for richer metadata — better descriptions,
subjects, and cover images — so your listings look their best.

## Order-file import

Already ordered from **Ingram** or **Edelweiss**? Import the order file and those
titles come in ready to sell — Bookhead reads each ISBN, enriches it, and adds it
to your catalog. See [Managing your inventory](./inventory.md) for how importing
works.

## Availability

Special orders and Ingram data are available on plans that include Ingram
integration. To turn this on for your store, email
[support@bookhead.net](mailto:support@bookhead.net).
